> Since the system must enforce compatibility between vehicle types and parking spot sizes, it’s important to clearly define the parking rules.
>
> A vehicle can use any spot that is at least as large as the vehicle:
>
> - **Bikes** (small) can park in **small, medium, or large** spots
> - **Cars** (medium) can park in **medium or large** spots
> - **Trucks** (large) can park in **large** spots only
>
> This prevents an oversized vehicle from taking an undersized spot. A smaller vehicle can still use a larger spot when nothing smaller is free, and the spot allocation strategy decides whether to favor an exact-size match so large spots stay open for larger vehicles.
