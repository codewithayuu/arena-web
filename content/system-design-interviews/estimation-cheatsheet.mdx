> A note before we begin: not every system design interview requires detailed capacity estimates. Check whether the interviewer wants estimates, and keep the math simple and rough. You do not want to spend valuable interview time doing calculator-style arithmetic.
>
> That said, getting a rough idea of the request rate and storage requirements almost always helps guide your design decisions.
