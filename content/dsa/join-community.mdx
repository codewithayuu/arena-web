Mindflare has an active community across a few places, and you can join in wherever you prefer. Here are the best ways to participate:

<ul>
  <li>
    Join the discussion on each lesson: Every topic has a comment section at the
    bottom. Ask questions, share your takeaways, and reply to others. If a
    comment helped you, leave a like so more people find it.
  </li>
  <li>
    Join our Discord: This is the best place for real-time discussions, help,
    and peer learning. Introduce yourself, share what you’re working on, and
    jump into ongoing conversations.
  </li>
  <li>
    Share feedback: We are working hard to improve the content every day. If
    something feels unclear, missing, or could be better, your feedback helps us
    make Mindflare the best place to learn software engineering and prepare for
    interviews.
  </li>
</ul>

See you inside.
