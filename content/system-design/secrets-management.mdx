Good secrets management is not just "keep it hidden." It answers four questions for every secret:

- **Where is the real value stored?**
- **Who is allowed to read it?**
- **How is it rotated?**
- **Who accessed it, and when?**

But before looking at the solution, let's look at how secrets usually leak.
