Most of the time, this works fine. But imagine the review service suddenly becomes slow. Calls that normally take 50 milliseconds now take 10 seconds.

1. Every product page request now waits for reviews while holding a thread.
2. Soon, all 200 threads can be stuck.
3. The product service cannot process anything else. Stock checks fail. Price lookups fail.
4. Even requests that do not need reviews cannot get a thread.

Reviews are not critical, but a slow review service has made the entire product service unavailable.

The slow review service is only part of the incident. The bigger problem is that every dependency shares the same pool of resources, so a non-critical feature was allowed to use capacity that everything else needs.

This is the problem the Bulkhead pattern solves.
