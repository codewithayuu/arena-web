Look up prices in a catalog containing "Wireless Mouse": 29.99 and "HDMI Cable": 14.99. Use a try/except/else statement in print_price: the try block reads catalog[product], the except KeyError branch reports the missing product, and the else branch prints a successful price formatted with two decimal places.

The program calls print_price for "Wireless Mouse" and "USB Hub". Print a found product as `<product>: $<price with two decimals>`, and print a missing product as `'<product>' is not in the catalog`.
